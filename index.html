About

Hi, I’m Mantha.

I started photographing what I cooked because the food kept looking better than my phone made it look. That turned into a feed, the feed turned into a community, and now it is the thing I spend my best hours on.

Everything you see here I cooked, styled and shot myself — mostly home food, mostly Indian, always the version I would actually eat.

Recipe development

Original recipes, tested until they work in a normal kitchen.

Food photography
